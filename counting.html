How Card Counting Works

This drill uses the Hi-Lo system, the same count the strategy deviations on the Simulation page use:

  • 2 – 6 → +1
  • 7 – 9 → 0
  • 10, J, Q, K, A → −1

Running count is just the sum of every value you've seen so far. True count is the running count divided by the number of decks remaining in the shoe — it's what actually tells you how favorable the shoe is, since a +4 running count means a lot more in a 1-deck shoe than a 6-deck one.

Pick a shoe size and a dealing pace, then watch the cards go by and keep a running tally in your head. Hit Stop & Guess whenever you're ready, and you'll be quizzed on both numbers.
