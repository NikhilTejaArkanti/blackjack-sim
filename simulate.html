The simulator always follows basic strategy from the chart exactly — every hit/stand/double/split/surrender decision is computed, never guessed, and strategy itself never looks at the count, unless you turn on strategy deviations above. A cut card is hidden at a random point (72–85% through the shoe, like a real casino); once it's reached the current hand finishes normally and the whole shoe is reshuffled before the next one. With everything off, every hand bets the base amount flat and the running/true count is shown purely for fun. Bet spread bets more as the count climbs. Deviations change a handful of specific hit/stand/double/split calls (and add insurance) once the count passes their threshold. Wonging skips hands entirely — advancing the shoe without betting — until the count turns favorable, so you only ever play favorable counts.
